FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Worko work without internet?

Yes. The desktop and Android apps keep a local store and an outbox. You can keep working; changes sync the moment you are back online.

How do updates work?

Every app checks for a new version when it starts. Windows updates with one click, macOS opens the new installer, Android asks once to install, and the web app offers a reload. Check any time in Appearance → About & updates.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when installing?

The builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway; on macOS right-click → Open the first time.

Is our firm's data separate from other firms?

Yes. Postgres row-level security isolates each firm, secrets are encrypted per tenant, and every change is written to a hash-chained audit log.

Is AI used on our data?

AI is off by default. When switched on it redacts personal data, and every suggestion needs a human to accept it.

Why can't I see WhatsApp, payments or e-sign?

Modules that need an outside account hide themselves until that account is connected by your administrator.
