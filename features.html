My day

One tap to finish.

The top of the dashboard shows only your work — overdue, today and the coming week.

  • Tick to finish — the row strikes through and slides away
  • Moon to move it to tomorrow
  • Undo from the toast if you slipped
  • Tasks with open checklist items open the task instead

Same card on Android, and it works offline. Go on — tick one.

What's inside

Ten modules. One source of truth.

Pick a module to see what it covers.

Modules that need an outside account (payment gateway, WhatsApp/SMS, e-sign, AI, calendar sync) hide themselves until that account is connected.
Android

Your practice, in your pocket.

The Android app has staff and client-portal modes, quick add, My day and offline reminders.

  • Local store + outbox — edits merge by field-level versions
  • Notification quiet hours and offline reminders
  • Client-portal mode: clients upload, answer and pay
